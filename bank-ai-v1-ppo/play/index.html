How to Play

  • 10 rounds. Two dice rolled each turn. All players share one pot.
  • Bank to lock in the current pot as your score.
  • Continue to risk it for a bigger pot.
  • Rolls 1-3 have immunity (auto-rolled, no banking): a 7 adds +70, doubles just add their sum.
  • After immunity: a 7 wipes the pot to 0 and ends the round. Doubles double the pot.
  • Highest total score after 10 rounds wins!
Keyboard: press B to bank, C to continue
